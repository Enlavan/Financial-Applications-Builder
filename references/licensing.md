# Personal use, access and deployment

Adapted from the supplied EODHD personal-use FAQ, reviewed 2026-10-25. The applicable published agreement governs. For an explicitly permitted personal case, additional confirmation is generally unnecessary; do not create an approval loop for ordinary private research.

## Personal use

One individual may use an appropriate personal plan for their own private investing/research: dashboards, screeners, valuations, backtests, paper trading, private alerts, automation and AI-assisted analysis. Each person needs their own account; sharing a key/account with friends, family or collaborators is not personal single-user access.

Private databases, normalized tables, backups and dated captures are allowed. Data may remain stored privately after cancellation; no general deletion deadline is imposed by this FAQ. Retention does not grant publication, redistribution, sharing or ongoing API access after the paid period.

Private use in a hosted AI service or on a cloud/VPS is permitted within these terms. Restrict access to the data to the individual; a server's public address alone is not a violation. Configure the chosen environment appropriately rather than asserting an unverified guarantee about third-party retention/access.

Open-source code may be shared without API keys, licensed data or private outputs; use synthetic fixtures. A free public dashboard, community bot, shared research service or AI product is not exempt merely because it has no revenue. Derived outputs are not automatically unrestricted.

## Commercial boundary

Use for a company, fund, employer, clients, a team or another person, and displaying/distributing data to others, needs suitable commercial rights. Arrange those rights before the change of use, not only after the first revenue. Password protection or invite-only access does not make a multi-user business personal.

Use [commercial pricing](https://eodhd.com/commercial-pricing) and sales@eodhistoricaldata.com for a use-specific quote. State datasets, purpose, audience, display/download/redistribution and expected access. Do not invent a development-plan entitlement or legal exemption.

## Classifying a build quickly (support practice)

| Situation | Classification |
|---|---|
| Private dashboard, screener, backtests, ML training, alerts to yourself, automation, private cloud DB/VPS, private Git repo of your own outputs | Personal |
| Using the data with an AI assistant or the EODHD MCP server through your own account, for your own analysis | Personal |
| Trading your own capital with signals built on the data | Personal |
| Showing data or charts to family members, friends, a club, a Discord/Telegram group, a newsletter or a public website — even free, invite-only or password-protected | Commercial (display to others) |
| A family company, your employer, a fund, or managing someone else's money | Commercial |
| A self-managed pension fund, trust or other borderline vehicle | Ask sales@eodhistoricaldata.com before building |
| A report or app for a single client | Commercial |
| An AI bot/service other people can query, or a model whose outputs expose the data to others | Commercial |
| Selling, reselling or relaying data or derived feeds | Redistribution agreement |

Practical notes: personal plans cannot be invoiced to a company; there are no extra exchange or
non-display fees on personal plans; commercial terms should be in place **before** launch. When the user's
plan changes, remember that plans union APIs but not limits, that the Fundamentals Data Feed has no prices,
and that Marketplace products are separate — see [plans-limits.md](plans-limits.md).

## Cancellation and plans

Cancellation stops renewal; access continues through the paid period. Afterwards another active subscription or Free access may remain. Cancellation is not account deletion. Standard monthly subscriptions have a one-month commitment; annual subscriptions have their annual billing term. The academic offer has a two-month minimum. Standard subscription payments are final/non-refundable under the applicable policy; do not promise a discretionary refund or change a plan without authorization.

Core plans and Marketplace products have different scopes/quotas. Fundamentals alone does not include historical EOD prices. Ordinary extra API calls do not extend Marketplace quotas. Prices and offerings may change: consult current official pages for a purchase decision rather than exporting historical support prices as permanent facts.

## Academic offer

For eligible individual students/academic users, the supplied offer is a 50% discount over 12 months, arranged after subscribing by credit card, not PayPal, and contacting support with appropriate proof. The first month is paid at the normal rate; the second month is free, making the first two months together half-price; the next ten months are half-price, then normal pricing resumes. This requires at least two months. It is not automatically applied by registering a school email, and a school email is not mandatory.

Acceptable proof depends on role: current student documentation for a student; current faculty/employment or equivalent academic affiliation for faculty, subject to support validation. Subscribe from the individual's own account and send documents to support@eodhistoricaldata.com. An individual discount does not authorize a class, laboratory or institution to share an account or redistribute a dataset.

## Trial and coverage expectations

Use supported demo samples or the user's existing entitled access for evaluation. Do not promise a general free paid-plan trial; specific Marketplace offers must be checked separately. Available coverage varies by instrument, interval, field and date. Delisted history is provided as is; completeness, future availability and restoration are not guaranteed. An expensive plan cannot recover nonexistent historical vintages or every missing bar.
